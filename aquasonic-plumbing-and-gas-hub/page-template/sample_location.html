Common jobs in Halls Head

What Halls Head homes call us about

Most calls fall into four kinds of work. Each one links to the full service page if you want more detail before you send an enquiry.

Water filtration

Cleaner water at every tap

Whole house filtration treats the water where it enters the home, so every tap and shower gets filtered water. Under-sink and benchtop systems suit people who only want filtered drinking water. We talk through what you want to remove and fit the system that matches. See water filtration.

Hot water

Replacing a system that has had its day

When a hot water system starts leaking or runs out early, it is usually time to look at a replacement. We supply and install gas, electric, solar, heat pump and continuous flow systems, with the valves, insulation and parts included in the install. See hot water installations.

Blocks and bursts

Blocked drains and burst pipes

A slow drain or a gurgling toilet often means a blockage further down the line. A burst pipe needs the water shut off first, then the repair. We find the cause, fix it and check the line is running clear. See blocks and bursts.

Leak detection

Finding hidden leaks without digging

A water bill that jumps or a damp patch with no obvious source usually points to a hidden leak. We find it without breaking concrete and give you the findings in writing, so you know exactly what needs fixing. See leak detection.

FAQs

What should I do if a pipe bursts in my Halls Head home?

Turn the water off at the meter, usually near the front boundary, then call +61 433 442 643. Emergency help is available 24/7, and we will talk you through keeping the damage down until we arrive.

Can you fit water filtration to an existing Halls Head home?

Yes. Whole house systems are fitted where the water enters the home, and under-sink or benchtop systems fit inside the kitchen. We look at your plumbing first and recommend the option that suits it.

Is my quote free?

Yes. Quotes are free with no obligation, so you can decide once you know what the job involves.

Can I pay for a hot water system over time?

Yes. We offer interest free payment options through Humm, Zip and Afterpay, so a new system can be paid off over time.
