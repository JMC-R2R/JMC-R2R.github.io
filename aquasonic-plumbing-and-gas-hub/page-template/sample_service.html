Emergency plumbing in Mandurah

Some plumbing problems cannot wait until the morning. A burst pipe can flood a floor in minutes, a blocked sewer can back up into the house and a gas leak is a safety risk. Our emergency service covers these jobs across Mandurah and the Peel region, day or night.

When you call, we ask what you can see and hear, help you make the home safe, then arrange the visit. On site we find the cause, make the repair or make it safe until the full repair can be done, and explain what happened.

Water

Burst pipes and major leaks

Turn the water off at the meter first, usually near the front boundary. That stops the damage while we are on the way. We then find where the pipe has failed, repair or replace the section and check the rest of the line for pressure loss.

Drains

Blocked drains and overflows

If a toilet, shower or floor waste is backing up, stop using water in the house until it is cleared. We clear the blockage, find out what caused it, whether that is roots, a collapse or a build up, and tell you if the line needs more work.

Gas

Gas leaks and appliances

If you smell gas, turn the gas off at the meter, open doors and windows, do not use switches or flames, and call us from outside. Our licensed gas fitters find the leak, make it safe and repair the fitting or appliance.

Hot water

No hot water

A hot water system that stops working is rarely dangerous, but it is hard to live without. We check whether it can be repaired, and if it needs replacing we supply and install a new gas, electric, solar, heat pump or continuous flow system.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Burst pipes, water you cannot turn off, sewage backing up into the house, a gas smell and a leak near electrics are emergencies. A dripping tap or a slow drain can usually wait for a normal booking.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

Turn the water off at the meter for a leak, or the gas off at the meter for a gas smell. Keep people away from water near power points, and move anything valuable away from the water.

Do you cover my suburb for emergencies?

We cover Mandurah and the Peel region, mainly around Mandurah. Call +61 433 442 643 with your suburb and we will tell you straight away whether we can come out.

Will I get a quote before the work starts?

Yes. Once we know what is wrong we explain the repair and the options before we go ahead, so there are no surprises.
