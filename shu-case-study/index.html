The brief

A brilliant restaurant that not enough people could find.

Shu serves modern Sichuan food that happens to be entirely plant-based — a 10-course degustation, all-vegan Yum Cha, private dining. The food converts sceptics. The problem was never the experience; it was getting enough of the right people through the door, predictably, every week.

STAGE 01 — DISCOVER

We found the searches that actually fill tables.

Instead of chasing vanity terms, we built the strategy around high-intent, ready-to-book searches that matched Shu's signature offerings.

  • Targeted "vegan yum cha", "vegetarian degustation" & "vegan fine dining" in Melbourne
  • Mapped each search to the right page — Yum Cha, degustation, bookings
  • Prioritised local, commercial intent over broad reach
SIGNATURE: ALL-VEGAN YUM CHA
STAGE 02 — BUILD

We turned the website into a booking engine.

SEO foundations plus content built around what Shu is genuinely known for — so the right pages started ranking and converting.

  • Optimised the Yum Cha & degustation landing pages
  • Built an explainer content layer — "what is yum cha", "degustation menu" — now ranking top-3 and feeding new diners
  • Clear paths to reserve, call and join the list on every page
10-COURSE VEGAN DEGUSTATION
STAGE 03 — AMPLIFY

A full Meta funnel — from "never heard of it" to "table booked".

We ran a structured Meta Ads funnel that introduced Shu to new diners, then followed up relentlessly with the people most likely to book.

  • Awareness video that earned 200k+ views of the food
  • Remarketing to bring warm diners back to reserve
  • Reservation & click-to-call ads driving direct enquiries
META: AWARENESS → REMARKETING → RESERVE
STAGE 04 — RE-ENGAGE & OWN

We turned a dead 9,000-contact list into Shu's most profitable channel.

Shu already sat on a database of ~9,000 contacts they assumed was a great asset — but open rates were tiny and it sat unused. We cleaned it, re-engaged the people who genuinely cared, and built offers worth opening for.

  • Cleaned & re-permissioned the 9,000-contact list down to a genuinely engaged core of ~500 buyers
  • Created email-exclusive offers — redeemed with a code on arrival, so every booking traces back to the list
  • Now private lessons, special wine dinners & events sell out from a single email send
OWNED LIST = REPEATABLE BOOKINGS

The journey

Start to finish, month by month.

Month 0 — Baseline

Visible for a few core terms but no content footprint, no paid funnel, an empty pipeline. Great food, not enough bookings.

Months 1–2 — Foundations

Keyword & intent strategy locked in and the signature pages optimised. We audited the 9,000-contact database, cleaned it, and began re-engaging the people who actually cared.

Months 2–4 — Momentum

Meta funnel goes live — awareness video, remarketing, reservation & call ads. The explainer content layer starts ranking and long-tail yum cha terms climb into the top 10. The re-engaged list starts converting.

Months 4–6 — Compounding

Ranks #1 on a typical day for vegan yum cha and owns the yum cha/degustation content cluster. Google Ads driving local store visits at $2.33. The re-engaged list — now ~500 active buyers — sells out private lessons, wine dinners and events from a single send.
