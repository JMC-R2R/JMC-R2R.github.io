Read-out

September eased back after August's event-led peak

  • Instagram interactions −64% on August (to 81) and views −42% to 3,707.
  • Instagram reached 1,985 accounts in September, 35% below August's event-led 3,041 and also below July's 2,256, so this is a genuine step back rather than a return to the pre-event level.
  • No trade event fell inside September. August had two, the Sydney Gluten-Free Expo and the Fine Food Australia lead-up, and without one the month settled lower: interactions 81 against August's 228, accounts engaged 59 against 149.
  • The month was front-loaded. 1 to 15 September delivered 1,411 of the 1,985 Instagram reach; the second half managed 574. The best week, 5 to 11 September, carried the pavlova, "seconds" and family-snacks posts and accounted for 998 on its own.
  • Fewer posts went out: 23 on Instagram against 31 in August. The late-month posts each reached between 18 and 36 accounts, so cadence and the strength of the closing fortnight are the first things to look at.
  • Facebook audience still grew. Page fans rose 19 to 1,488 (August added 13), even though organic impressions eased to 3,820 from 5,161 and post engagements to 45 from 92.
  • LinkedIn held a steady weekly rhythm with four newsletter posts (7, 14, 20 and 27 September), but impressions fell to 103 from August's 198. The 27 September post on trust in food distribution did best: 32 impressions, 2 clicks, 6.3% engagement.
  • Next up: Food & Hospitality Queensland and the Café & Coffee Experience in Brisbane (Stand M7, 12 to 13 October), already being promoted from 29 September. On the August pattern, an event month should lift October.
  • Audience: +17 Instagram followers this month and +19 Facebook fans vs August.
  • Next lever: comments & saves remain the low numbers — stronger CTAs and questions in captions will lift two-way engagement.

Methodology: Organic (non-paid) Instagram, Facebook & LinkedIn data pulled live via Windsor.ai for 1 March to 30 September 2026 (LinkedIn from campaign launch). "Accounts reached" and "Content views" are the sum of daily account-level figures and may count repeat viewers across days. Follower counts are end-of-month figures. Instagram's total (939) is the live snapshot taken on 2 October, as this report was run a day late; Windsor.ai records no new follows on 1 October, so it stands as the closing September figure. The net gain of 17 is measured against August's 922; Windsor.ai's daily series records 27 gross new follows for 2 to 30 September (1 September falls outside its 30-day window). August comparison figures are slightly higher than in the August report (Instagram reach 3,041 vs 3,016, Facebook impressions 5,161 vs 5,143, page fans 1,469 vs 1,468) because Windsor.ai restated 31 August after that report ran. LinkedIn's 4 new followers are LinkedIn's organic follower gains for September; the page total is 26. Post thumbnails saved locally so the report stays intact over time.
