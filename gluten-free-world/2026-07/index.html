Read-out

July: what the numbers show

  • Instagram interactions −60% on June (to 146) and views +28% to 5,561.
  • Instagram reached 2,256 accounts in July, the highest month since the campaign began and 62% ahead of June.
  • The interaction drop is a June artefact, not a July decline. June logged 366 interactions, but 21 June alone accounted for 284 of them (273 likes from 268 accounts in a single day). Excluding that one day, June was 82 interactions and July's 146 is roughly 78% ahead on a like-for-like basis.
  • Partner and creator content drove the reach. The three highest-reaching posts were all reshares: a Clay Health and Organics shelf tour (198 accounts reached), a Foodbank Victoria warehouse visit (194) and a Coeliac at Home cinnamon scroll reel (162). Owned brand posts peaked at 215. Reposting stockist and creator content is the cheapest reach lever available.
  • Facebook was the quiet winner: 4,218 organic impressions, up 59% on June and the strongest month of the campaign, on 29 new page fans.
  • LinkedIn needs attention. Impressions fell to 236 from 697 across five posts, returning one click and one new follower. The B2B track is not yet earning its place in the mix.
  • Audience: +41 Instagram followers this month and +29 Facebook fans vs June.
  • Next lever: comments & saves remain the low numbers — stronger CTAs and questions in captions will lift two-way engagement.

Methodology: Organic (non-paid) Instagram, Facebook & LinkedIn data pulled live via Windsor.ai for 1 March to 31 July 2026 (LinkedIn from campaign launch). "Accounts reached" and "Content views" are the sum of daily account-level figures and may count repeat viewers across days. Follower counts are end-of-month figures. Windsor.ai only serves Instagram follower gains for the trailing 30 days, so July's Instagram total (852) is the live 10 August snapshot (861) less the 9 followers gained 1 to 9 August, and July's new-follower figure is that total less the 811 recorded on 1 July. Post thumbnails saved locally so the report stays intact over time.
