Read-out

October: two trade events lift engagement, but reach lags behind

  • Instagram interactions +152% on September (to 204) and views +72% to 6,364 — the audience is engaging more, not just seeing more.
  • Instagram reached 2,100 accounts in October, up 6% on September's 1,985 but still well below August's event-led peak of 3,041 — audience growth was modest even though engagement surged.
  • Two trade events landed in October — Food & Hospitality Queensland in Brisbane (around 11 to 13 October) and the Melbourne Gluten-Free Expo (17 to 18 October) — and it shows: interactions jumped to 204 from September's 81 (+152%) and views to 6,364 from 3,707 (+72%).
  • Reach grew far more slowly than engagement. Accounts reached rose only 6% to 2,100, so most of October's lift came from people who had already seen Gluten Free World content engaging more, not from a larger audience. The back half of the month (16 to 31 October, expo week and after) carried 1,186 of the month's reach against 914 in the first half.
  • One day skews the views total. On 21 October, Instagram views spiked to 934 against a typical day of 100 to 300, while reach that day stayed ordinary at 91 — a single post was replayed heavily by an already-reached audience rather than freshly discovered, which inflates the monthly views figure.
  • Facebook kept growing but more slowly than September. Page fans rose 7 to 1,495 (September added 19), while organic impressions improved to 4,628 from 3,820 and post engagements to 80 from 45.
  • LinkedIn's final day of October could not be retrieved. Windsor.ai returned no row for 31 October after three attempts (the initial full pull, a narrow 3-day re-pull, and a 2-minute wait and retry), so October's LinkedIn impressions (100), clicks (2) and follower gain (0) cover 1 to 30 October only and understate the true monthly total slightly.
  • Next up: no November trade event has been confirmed yet, so cadence and the strength of recent content — not an event calendar — will need to carry momentum into next month.
  • Audience: +66 Instagram followers this month and +8 Facebook fans vs September.
  • Next lever: comments & saves remain the low numbers — stronger CTAs and questions in captions will lift two-way engagement.

Methodology: Organic (non-paid) Instagram, Facebook & LinkedIn data pulled live via Windsor.ai for 1 March to 31 October 2026 (LinkedIn from campaign launch). "Accounts reached" and "Content views" are the sum of daily account-level figures and may count repeat viewers across days. Follower counts are end-of-month figures. Instagram's total (1,005) is the live snapshot taken on 1 November, as this report ran on schedule on the 1st, so it stands as the closing October figure. The net gain of 66 is measured against September's closing figure of 939; Windsor.ai's daily series (which only covers the trailing 30 days, excluding today) records 75 gross new follows for 2 to 31 October (1 October falls outside its 30-day window). LinkedIn's daily series returned no row for 31 October despite three retrieval attempts, so LinkedIn's October impressions (100), clicks (2) and new followers (0) cover 1 to 30 October only; the company page total of 26 followers is unchanged and was confirmed with a fresh live pull.
