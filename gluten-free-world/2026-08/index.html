Read-out

August was the campaign's strongest month, and two trade events are why

  • Instagram interactions +55% on July (to 227) and views +13% to 6,265 — the audience is engaging more, not just seeing more.
  • Instagram reached 3,016 accounts in August, the strongest month since the campaign began and 34% ahead of July.
  • Two trade events carried the month. The Coeliac Australia Gluten-Free Expo (Royal Randwick, Sydney, 15-16 August) and the Fine Food Australia announcement (Melbourne, 31 August to 3 September) shaped the content calendar. The four-day expo window of 13-16 August alone delivered 767 of the month's 3,016 Instagram reach and half of its new followers.
  • Instagram follower growth was the best of the campaign: a net +70 for the month, 852 to 922, against +41 in July. Twenty of those arrived on 15 August, the expo's opening day.
  • Engagement rose on real spread, not a single spike. Interactions reached 227 against July's 146, and accounts engaged 148 against 88. Unlike June, no single day distorts the total - August's busiest day contributed 22 interactions.
  • Facebook also hit a campaign high: 5,143 organic impressions, up 22% on July, with page fans up 12 to 1,468.
  • LinkedIn softened and needs attention. 198 impressions against July's 236 and June's 697. Only four posts went out, all long-form thought-leadership, and the 9 August post carried 127 of the 198 impressions on its own. The other three drew 71 between them. Posting cadence is the first thing to look at.
  • Audience: +70 Instagram followers this month and +12 Facebook fans vs July.
  • Next lever: comments & saves remain the low numbers — stronger CTAs and questions in captions will lift two-way engagement.

Methodology: Organic (non-paid) Instagram, Facebook & LinkedIn data pulled live via Windsor.ai for 1 March to 31 August 2026 (LinkedIn from campaign launch). "Accounts reached" and "Content views" are the sum of daily account-level figures and may count repeat viewers across days. Follower counts are end-of-month figures. Instagram's total (922) is the live snapshot taken on 1 September, which is the closing August figure; the month's net gain of 70 is measured against July's 852. Windsor.ai's daily series records 77 gross new follows for August, the 7-follow difference being unfollows. LinkedIn's 31 August row was missing from the first data pull and retrieved on a retry, so August's LinkedIn impressions are 198, not the 180 an incomplete pull would show.
