Your weekly ads check-in

What this is: every Monday, tell us what last week's Google Ads brought in. It takes about a minute.

Why it matters: the ads show us clicks and calls, but only you know which ones turned into real jobs. Your answers show what the ads actually earn you for every dollar spent, so we can put more budget into the jobs that pay and cut what doesn't.

Only you and the Ready to Rank team see your answers.

Which week?
1How many jobs came from Google Ads?

Count every enquiry from the ads that you quoted on or booked in, won or not.

2What type of jobs were they, and what was each worth?

The value is what you quoted or charged, including GST. A rough figure is fine.

3How many did you win?

Won means the customer said yes, even if the work isn't finished yet.

4What's the value of the jobs you won?

Tick the ones you won and check the amount.
