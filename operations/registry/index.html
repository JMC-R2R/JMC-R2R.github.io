How to read this

Onboarding is the twelve-point definition of done. A build or an ads engagement is scored on the five that apply to it. A client only counts as onboarded when every engagement they are on has passed. State, score, blockers and next thing are worked out from the data and can never be typed in.

Next thing is the oldest open blocker on the access register, with the person who owns it and how long it has been sitting. Ticking rows there is what moves the score.

Mark complete closes a client by hand when the work is finished but the register was never ticked. It records who and why, and the row is tagged closed by hand. Cells marked edit on hover can be changed by the register writers; every change is logged.
