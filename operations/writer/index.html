Blog writer

Writes blogs and pages through Surfer once the client approves the topic. Each new article uses 1 Surfer credit and counts towards the client's monthly cap. Rewrites are free.

Writing instructions

Voice, structure, calls to action, links, photos and style. Plain English. The writer follows these for every blog, then each client's own rules on top (set under Clients).

When and how much

The writer runs every hour at :22. Outside these days and hours it skips the run. The default cap applies to any client without their own cap.

Takes effect on the next run.

LinkedIn writer

Writes every LinkedIn article ahead of time, with its promo post and banner, so the client approves topic and article together. Uses no Surfer credits.

Writing instructions

Article, meta title and description, promo post and hashtags. The client's own rules apply on top.

When and how much

Word counts are for articles made from a blog. A monthly series uses its own word counts. The writer runs every hour at :52.

Takes effect on the next run.

Release day

On release day, everything QC-approved goes to the client to review, and each contact gets their own "ready to review" email. When every blog in a month is live on their site, they get a "blogs are live" email. The email layout and logos stay as they are. You set the day, what goes out and the wording.

Clients

Turn each writer on or off per client, set their monthly blog cap, and keep what the writer knows about them up to date. Their rules here apply on top of the writing instructions.

Facts the writer may use

The only personal facts the writer can state (years in business, licences, team, awards, prices). Each one needs a source, e.g. "kickoff call 03/10". With no facts, the writer says nothing personal.

Pages to link to

The only pages on the client's site the writer may link to. Full URLs.

Client rules

Anything specific to this client. These override the general writing instructions, e.g. "Residential only, never mention car lockouts".

Waiting to be written

Approved blog and page topics, and queued LinkedIn pieces. Write next moves a piece to the front of the queue for the next run. A blog still needs a credit under the cap.

Written

Rewrite sends a piece back to the writer with your note. Same as Send back in the hub, and free.

Fixed rules

These are built into the writers and stay on whatever the instructions say. If an instruction conflicts with one of them, the writer follows the rule below.

Always Locked

  • One Surfer credit per article, ever. Never Surfer's AI Article, Auto-Optimize or paid recommendations.
  • Monthly caps: you can set 1 to 10 blogs per client. Anything above 10 needs Jose.
  • Personal facts (years, licences, team, awards, prices, reviews, promises) only from the client's sourced facts. No source, no claim.
  • No invented bylines. Only the client's real photos, never someone else's.
  • Links only to the client's listed pages and their website. Outside links are recorded for QC to check.
  • Nothing reaches a client until Janice or Jhen QC-approve it and it's released.
  • Release emails go only to the client's own contacts on file, one email per person.
