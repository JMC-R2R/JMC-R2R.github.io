What she does

Everything below is live today. She can read but not change anything, apart from the one email flow, which needs two confirmations.

Ask her

Answers questions from live data

Tag @Mabel in a channel or DM her. Client status, content progress, onboarding, dashboards. She reads the agency database and never invents a number.

Threads

Follows a conversation

Once she's in a thread she replies without being tagged. She stays quiet on "thanks", emoji and messages aimed at someone else.

Client email

Sends blogs for approval

Jose or Jhen ask, she previews, "send", then type confirm send <Client>. Each contact gets their own branded email. Asking counts as QC sign-off.

Routines

Posts on a schedule

Reports and checks she runs on her own, set up in Routines. She also posts for the content routines (QC requests, client comments, approvals).

Guardrails

What she never does

Shows Ready to Rank's fees or budgets, mentions one client in another client's channel, tags Aljim, or edits anything. Ad spend and cost per lead appear only in ads channels.

Brain

Picks a model per request

Quick questions go to Haiku, normal ones to Sonnet, hard ones to Opus. Say "deep dive" or "use opus" to force Opus. Near the cap she steps down a level.

Instructions

How Mabel talks and what she knows about the team and clients, in plain English. Edit a draft, test it to your DM, then make it live. Every version is kept, so you can always go back.

Personality and tone

How she sounds. e.g. Warm but brief. Sign off with nothing. Use "we" for Ready to Rank.

Team facts

Client notes

One paragraph per client, starting with the client's name. She uses a client's notes only in that client's channel or when asked about them.

House rules

Habits to always or never do. e.g. When someone asks about a blog, always give its live link.

The test saves your draft first, then she answers in your Slack DMs using the draft. Nobody else sees it.
