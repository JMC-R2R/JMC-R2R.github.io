How we work

The bits that decide whether a quote holds up.

Bundles are always cheaper than the sum of the parts — and the client will do that sum, so the arithmetic has to be shown honestly. The bundle rate holds only while both services run; drop one and each reverts to its standalone price. Minimum term follows the longest component.

Ad spend

Management fees never include ad spend. The client pays Google or Meta direct. Quoting a combined number is the fastest way to a difficult conversation in month two.

Minimum terms

Paid ads run a 3-month minimum — anything shorter doesn't clear the learning phase. Everything else is 6 months. SEO on a 3-month term is a refund conversation waiting to happen.

What isn't on this page

Anything showing POA has no agreed price yet. If a client needs one of those, message Jose rather than estimating — a number quoted here becomes the number they expect.

Launching Meta ads for your client

What we need from you and from the client, and when. Follow this and every campaign you sell goes live four weeks after the onboarding call.

Name every campaign in the signed scope. If the scope says two campaigns, we build two. If a campaign was only mentioned in conversation, it won't make it into the build. One line per campaign, with its goal.

1. What you send us with every lead

Before the onboarding call, so the brief is ready on day one.

ItemWhy we need it
Signed scope listing every campaign by nameEach campaign is built, tracked and approved as its own line
For each campaign: the goal, the product or service, and the locationsEnquiries, bookings or online sales each need a different setup, landing page and audience
The client's main contact, and who approves the adsApprovals go by email to one named person
Brand kit: logo, fonts and coloursKeeps the ads and the landing page consistent with your creative
Filming dateSets the launch date
Website or Shopify login, if they sell onlineNeeded to install tracking where the sale happens

2. What the client does on the onboarding call

Done live with us on the call, so none of it has to be chased later.

  • Adds their own credit card to the Meta ad accountThe client pays Meta directly for ad spend
  • Completes Meta business verificationUnverified accounts are held back on spend and lead forms
  • Verifies their website domain in MetaSeparate from business verification. Both are needed
  • Gives Ready to Rank partner access to the ad account, Facebook Page, Instagram account and pixel
  • Names the person who approves ads

3. What you film and deliver

  • Hooks and scripts agreed with us before the shootSo the videos match the ad copy the client approves
  • Creative for every campaign in the scopeEach campaign needs its own videos
  • Filmed by day 14, edited and delivered by day 21
  • Vertical 9:16 for Reels and Stories, plus 4:5 for Feed
  • One folder per campaign, with each file named for the ad it belongs to

4. What Ready to Rank delivers

  • Campaign strategy and build for every campaign in the scope
  • Ad copy, headlines and calls to action, sent to the client for approval by email
  • Landing pageIncluded with Meta Ads Management
  • Tracking, tested before launchPixel and conversions set up, a test lead sent through to the CRM, and every lead tagged with the ad it came from
  • Ad previews sent to the client for approval before launch
  • Final pre-launch check, then launch
  • Monthly performance report after launch

6. What pushes a launch back

  • No card on the account. The ads can't spend.
  • Verification left until later. Meta limits spend and lead forms until it's done.
  • A campaign that isn't in the scope. It doesn't get built.
  • Videos after day 21. Launch moves back by the same number of days.
  • Approvals waiting on the client. Nothing goes live without their written OK.
